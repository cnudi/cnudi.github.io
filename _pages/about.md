# Data Intelligence Lab

We are the **Data Intelligence Lab (DILAB)** at the School of Computing and AI, Chungnam National University.

Our research focuses on **graph machine learning, trustworthy AI, GraphRAG and LLMs, and recommender systems**, with broader interests in data mining and knowledge-enhanced AI.


# 🔬 Research

- **Graph Machine Learning**  
  Representation learning and machine learning for complex graph-structured data

- **Trustworthy AI**  
  Fairness, uncertainty quantification, and reliability of machine learning models

- **GraphRAG & LLMs**  
  Graph-enhanced retrieval, reasoning, and knowledge augmentation for large language models

- **Recommender Systems & Information Retrieval**  
  Learning and retrieval methods for personalized and knowledge-rich information systems
