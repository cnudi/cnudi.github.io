# Research

Our research focuses on graph machine learning and its integration with modern AI technologies for learning, reasoning, and solving real-world problems.

---

## Graph Representation Learning

We develop graph representation learning methods for complex relational data, including heterogeneous, signed, dynamic, and higher-order graphs.

**Representative Publications**
- *Multi-Hyperbolic Space-based Heterogeneous Graph Attention Network* — ICDM 2024
- *Hyperbolic Heterogeneous Graph Attention Networks* — WWW 2024
- *CR-SGCN: Unsupervised Signed Community Detection via Conductance Regularization* — CIKM 2025

---

## Graph + LLM

We combine graph-structured knowledge with large language models for retrieval, reasoning, and representation learning, with recent interests in GraphRAG and text-attributed graphs.

**Representative Publications**
- *Who Should Teach? Confidence-Aware Dual-Teacher Learning for Few-Shot Node Classification on Text-Attributed Graphs* — CIKM 2026
- *StAR: Adaptive Structure-Aware Reranking for Semantic-Structural Alignment in GraphRAG* — SIGIR 2026
- *Metadata Meets LLMs: Constructing Knowledge-Rich Citation Networks with CoT-Enhanced Representations* — WSDM 2026
- *GRAIL: Graph Retrieval-Augmented In-Context Learning for Node Classification in Real-World Textual-Attributed Graphs* — EDBT 2025

---

## Graph Applications

We apply graph learning to a wide range of real-world problems, including recommender systems, information retrieval, traffic forecasting, anomaly detection, and network analysis.

**Representative Publications**
- *DisCoRec: Disentangled Conformity-aware Recommendation with LLM-Guided Multi-View Learning* — SIGIR 2026
- *Sequence-aware Adaptive Graph Convolutional Recurrent Networks for Traffic Forecasting* — Knowledge-Based Systems 2025
- *LLM-powered Scene Graph Representation Learning for Image Retrieval via Visual Triplet-based Graph Transformation* — Expert Systems with Applications 2025
- *SiReN: Sign-Aware Recommendation Using Graph Neural Networks* — IEEE TNNLS 2024
